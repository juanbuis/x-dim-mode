New
• Made for the redesigned X. X rebuilt its whole site in November, and Dim now covers all of it: handles and timestamps, Chat bubbles, notifications, settings, Creator Studio and the lines between posts match your theme again.
• Dim is back as an option in X's own Appearance settings, between Light and Dark.
• Switching Dim on now puts X in Dim right away, even if X was set to Light. Pick "Use system setting" in X and you get light by day and Dim at night.
• Button color (Extras) — Post and Follow buttons in white, your theme color, or any color you pick.
• Hide Grok (Extras) — removes Grok from the sidebar, posts, profiles and the floating button.
• Sidebar (Extras) — choose which items show in X's sidebar.
• Every Extra has a small info icon that says what it does.

Changed
• The popup, Extras and welcome screens are redesigned to match the new X.

Fixed
• The popup could say Dim was on while X stayed un-dimmed. If X itself is on Light, the popup now says so and offers a "Switch to Dim" button.
• The bird logo, "Tweet, not Post", the classic image grid, "Copy link first" and "Start on Following" work on the redesigned X.

Something broken? There's a "Report a problem" link in Extras.
