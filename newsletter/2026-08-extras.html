Six small things hiding in X Dim Mode

Hey,

Thanks for installing X Dim Mode, and for signing up to hear from us. We promised a few emails a year and we meant it, so this is the first one.

Here's the thing we keep hearing: most people never find half of what's in there.

Which is fair enough. You came for the dark blue background — that's the whole reason it exists. But if you click the X Dim Mode icon in your toolbar, there's a row called Extras, and behind it are six optional toggles. They're all off by default, so nothing changes unless you decide you want it to:
Classic bird logo — the bird, back where the X is
Classic font — Helvetica Neue, what Twitter used before Chirp
Classic favicon — the little blue bird in your tab again
“Tweet”, not “Post” — the old wording, right down to the compose button
Start on Following — opens on Following instead of For You
Classic image grid — multi-image posts back in a grid, instead of a carousel that hides half of them

Turn on whatever you like the look of. Leave the rest alone.
 

If something looks off

X redesigns things constantly, and every so often that breaks something here. There's a “Report a problem” link right in the popup — we read all of them, and fixes usually go out within a few days. Honestly the fastest way to get something sorted.

Thanks for using it. It's nice knowing this many people still want their old Twitter back.
